# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site, deployed by GitHub Actions to GitHub Pages. Canonical domain thedna.casa; www redirects to the apex.

## Users

Friends and family of Dan and Alexis who were sent the link. They arrive curious and friendly, usually on a phone, and stay for a minute or two.

## Product Purpose

A personal home on the web for one household: Dan, Alexis, Milly (dog) and Penny (cat). It says hello warmly and introduces who lives here. Success is a visitor leaving having met all four.

## Positioning

The site is the household itself. Nothing on it could belong to another family: the names, the pets, the couple's own colors and motifs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v1 is one landing page: hero, the four members, one or two photos with a short welcome, footer with the year.
- Nothing to sell, no sign-up, no contact link, no CMS, no analytics, no search, no RSS.
- Content is edited as Markdown in the repo by Dan and Claude.
- Privacy: public, first names only, no location clues, photo EXIF/GPS stripped.
- Deferred: events, Christmas cards, trips, posts (see `.scratch/TODO.md`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "The DNA Casa", set in all caps as THE DNA CASA; subtitle "Dan & Alexis".
- English, with one or two Spanish phrases (e.g. "Bienvenidos").
- Binding motifs from the brief: Oaxacan architecture, terracotta arches (Alexis), Salamander green #2F3E3E (Dan), a folk-art sol y luna as the one astronomy element.
- Light page overall; no black backgrounds.
- Excluded: sugar skulls, sombreros, papel picado, serape stripes, saguaro, rainbow palettes, kitschy sun faces.

## Evidence on Hand

- No photos yet; placeholders until Dan adds 1-2 files to `src/assets/photos/`. Pets may appear in them.
- One-liners for each member are not written yet; do not present invented ones as final.

## Product Principles

1. Meeting the four of them is the point; everything else frames it.
2. Personal over polished: hand-made marks beat stock components.
3. Privacy first: nothing that locates the household.
4. Small and finished beats large and partial.
